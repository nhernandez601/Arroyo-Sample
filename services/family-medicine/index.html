Family Medicine

Our team of family medicine doctors, internists, general practice doctors, nurse practitioners, and a physician assistant cares for your whole family. This includes our Diabetes Case Management Program.

Call to make an appointment

What we offer

  • Annual physical and wellness exams
  • Cholesterol and blood sugar screenings
  • Cancer screenings: prostate (PSA), colon, mammograms, and cervical (Pap)
  • Family planning
  • STD/STI testing and treatment
  • HIV testing
  • TB testing
  • Psychosocial assessments
  • Chronic disease management
  • Free blood pressure checks
  • Senior care: physical, vision, and hearing exams; flu, COVID, pneumococcal, and RSV vaccines; Medicare help
  • Diabetes classes, one-on-one counseling, and free glucometers
  • Laboratory, pharmacy, and specialty referrals
Check with your health plan for any rewards for completing a wellness exam. For diabetes classes and our Case Management Program, call (323) 254-5221.

Who it's for

Adults, seniors, and the whole family.

How to make an appointment

Call any of our clinics to make an appointment. Current patients can also request appointments through the Patient Portal.
