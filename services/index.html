Our services

We offer medical, dental, optometry, behavioral health, imaging, and pharmacy services for all ages: babies, children, teens, adults, and seniors. Many services are free, including health education, flu vaccine clinics, children's immunizations, and our annual health fairs.

New patients: (323) 254-5221 ext. 9022

Family Medicine

Checkups, screenings, and care for ongoing conditions like diabetes.

Pediatrics

Well-child visits, school and sports physicals, and free immunizations.

Prenatal Care / OB-GYN

Care from your first pregnancy test through delivery and after.

Women's Health

Exams, Pap tests, mammograms, and family planning.

Dental

Cleanings, fillings, root canals, dentures, and extractions.

Optometry

Eye exams and a full optical shop for glasses.

Behavioral Health

Counseling, case management, and links to community resources.

Podiatry

Foot exams, diabetic foot checks, and ingrown toenails.

Imaging

X-rays, mammography, and ultrasound.

Enhanced Care Management

A care manager to help Medi-Cal members with complex needs.

Referrals & Pharmacy

Help with specialist referrals and discounted prescriptions.

Services vary by location. Please call to confirm and schedule.
Prevention first

We believe in preventive care

  • Everyone should have a complete physical exam every year.
  • Moms-to-be should get prenatal care from the first trimester through postpartum.
  • Every child should get vaccines on time.
  • Every woman should have access to a mammogram when needed.
  • Children, teens, seniors, and people with chronic illness or special needs should get a flu vaccine every year.
