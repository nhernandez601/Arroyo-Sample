NOV21 Free class

Basics of Diabetes Class

Diabetes can be prevented and kept under control. Join us to learn what diabetes is, the different types, your blood sugar goals, and four areas of self-care: emotional well-being, physical activity, medication, and healthy eating.

  • Saturday, November 21, 2026
  • 9–11am in English · 2–4pm in Spanish
  • Highland Park · 6000 N. Figueroa St.
  • Irma or Cristina: (323) 225-0024 ext. 4066

Free Flu Shots

Protect yourself and your family this flu season. Insurance is not required, and flu shots are available for everyone 6 months and older. No appointment needed, while supplies last.
