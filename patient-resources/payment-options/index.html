Payment options & insurance

Patients with or without insurance, Medi-Cal, and Medicare are welcome. We provide care regardless of ability to pay, and we work with you to make paying simple.

Pay a bill in the Patient Portal

Ways we help

  • Sliding Fee Scale discounts and payment plans for those who qualify (see notice)
  • Bilingual financial screeners guide you through eligibility for free or discounted services
  • Enrollment help for Medi-Cal, Covered California, and Medicare Advantage, plus help choosing a doctor and health plan
  • Uninsured and underinsured patients can ask for a Good Faith Estimate of costs (No Surprises Act)
  • We accept cash, credit cards, checks, and money orders

Free for our patients

  • Health education, including bilingual diabetes classes
  • Nutrition education and counseling
  • Annual adult and children's health fair
  • Pregnancy tests
  • Childhood vaccines (VFC) and adult vaccines (VFA)
  • Flu and pneumonia vaccine clinics
  • Health screenings at special events
  • Help with specialty referral appointments
